I love the feeling of remoteness, roughness and adventure in the mountains. I enjoy planning alpine adventures and exploring less frequented areas and routes. In winter I enjoy ski-touring, in summer I enjoy ridge scrambling and light alpine climbs. My strengths are fast movement in alpine, rocky terrain up to UIAA grade 4 and planning and navigation in pathless, alpine environment. Recently, I started paragliding.
